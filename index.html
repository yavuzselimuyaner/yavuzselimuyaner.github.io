Data Science / ML

Research Trend Analysis via Unsupervised Topic Modeling

Senior thesis and my most extensive project. Collected metadata for 4,003 IEEE TIFS articles (2015–2025) and mapped them into 13 subfields with BERTopic (SBERT + UMAP + HDBSCAN), benchmarked against LDA and NMF. Designed a three-part diversity index covering topic breadth per paper, the authors’ topic history, and institutional distance between co-authors (Shannon entropy, Rao–Stirling), then analysed how it relates to citation counts with regression. Early results suggest that focused papers from widely collaborating teams tend to be cited more. The write-up is still in progress.
